ABOUT AKAAR

Two minds.
One creative vision.

Akaar Studio is an architectural practice led by Ar. Ishwari Dasari and Ar. Chirantan. Working across Solapur, Karad and Maharashtra, we bring conceptual rigour and material sensitivity to private residences, commercial spaces and housing.

We listen before we draw. We question before we commit. And we stay by your side from the first sketch to the final nail.
